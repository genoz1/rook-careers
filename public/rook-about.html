About ROOK

We knew there had to be a smarter way.

ROOK was born from firsthand experience with the challenges of finding the right opportunity in medical and animal health sales.

Built by someone who knows the job search from the inside.

ROOK started with a simple frustration: finding a great medical or animal health sales job shouldn't be this difficult.

After years working in medical diagnostics, healthcare sales, and animal health sales, ROOK's founder unexpectedly found himself back in the job market.

What he discovered was frustrating.

Major job boards had become overwhelming. The same positions appeared repeatedly. Some listings were outdated or no longer accepting applications. Legitimate opportunities could attract enormous applicant pools almost immediately.

For professionals with specialized experience, spending hours searching through thousands of unrelated positions wasn't just frustrating. It was inefficient.

There had to be a smarter way. That's why ROOK was created.

The Problem

Job searching became a numbers game.

Traditional job platforms are designed to serve virtually everyone. ROOK isn't.

Experienced professionals in specialized industries often encounter duplicate listings, outdated postings, unclear application status, irrelevant recommendations, and legitimate opportunities that attract enormous applicant pools almost immediately.

ROOK was created around a different idea: don't make professionals search through more jobs — help them identify the right ones.

The ROOK Difference

We go closer to the source.

Instead of relying solely on the same job-board feeds everyone else uses, ROOK searches the places that matter most: the employers themselves. ROOK identifies opportunities directly from company career sites across medical device, diagnostics, pharmaceutical, biotechnology, healthcare technology, veterinary, animal health, and related industries. We also invite recruiters and employers to post opportunities directly to ROOK.

The goal is a more focused collection of opportunities built specifically for professionals pursuing careers in the industries we know.
