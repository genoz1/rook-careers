About ROOK

We built the tool we wished existed during our own job search.

ROOK exists because searching medical and veterinary sales openings one job board at a time is a bad use of a professional salesperson's time — and it doesn't get easier the more experienced you are.

Why we started here

ROOK began with a very specific, unglamorous problem: a medical and veterinary sales professional going through a job search, manually checking a dozen sites a day, and still missing roles that were a genuine fit.

Generic AI job tools already exist. What didn't exist was one that actually understood the difference between reference-lab diagnostics and point-of-care diagnostics, or between veterinary distribution and veterinary capital equipment — distinctions that matter enormously to hiring managers in this industry and are invisible to a keyword search.

What We Believe

The industry deserves better than keyword search.

Explained, not just scored

A match percentage with no explanation is a number, not information. Every match shows exactly why it fits and what might not.

Direct from the source

Roles are sourced from employer career sites and applicant-tracking systems directly — not recycled from other job boards.

Never fabricated

Tailored résumés reorganize and emphasize your real experience. ROOK never invents a job, a skill, or an accomplishment.

How ROOK actually works

01

One profile, once

Upload a résumé and set your preferences a single time — territory, compensation floor, travel tolerance, industries you'll consider.

02

Continuous discovery

ROOK checks employer career sources on a schedule, not on request — so opportunities are waiting for you, not the other way around.

03

Weighted, explained matching

Scores combine deterministic factors (territory, comp, required experience) with semantic similarity — never a bare AI guess.

04

You apply, on your terms

ROOK prepares a tailored application package; you decide what to send and where, on the employer's own application page.
