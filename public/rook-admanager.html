Budget Flow — How Money Shifts
Conversion metric: Platform-reported conversions (signups/onboarding starts attributed to each ad). CPA = spend ÷ conversions.
Scale up (+20%): When CPA < $14 (70% of target) — campaign is profitable, give it more budget.
Scale down (−15%): When CTR < 0.1% with 5,000+ impressions — creative fatigue, reduce waste.
Recommend pause: When CPA > $60 (3× target) — spending too much per conversion, flag for review.
Combined cap: Total daily budget across all campaigns is $25/day. App can shift up to 20% ($5) between campaigns to follow performance. Hard ceiling is $30/day — never exceeded.
Minimum data: 20 clicks AND $5 spend required before any budget action fires.
Schedule: Runs daily at 8 AM ET via DigitalOcean scheduled job (not yet enabled — see below).
