Questions

What counts as a "qualified" match for the guarantee?

A qualified match meets your stated hard requirements — geography or territory, acceptable travel, industry/role preferences, and compensation minimum when the posting discloses salary — and is an active, open role at the time it's presented to you.

Is there a free trial?

There's no crippled free tier, but the 30-day guarantee works the same way — if the matching doesn't hold up for your specific background, you get your first month back.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime from your account settings, effective at the end of the current billing period.

Do you offer team or employer pricing?

Employer and recruiter access is priced separately and isn't included in the candidate Professional plan. Reach out from the For Employers page for details.
