Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026
This policy is provided as a good-faith description of ROOK's actual cookie usage. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and should not be treated as final, legally binding language until it has been.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember information about your visit.

2. How ROOK Uses Cookies

ROOK uses cookies for one primary purpose: to keep you securely signed in to your account between visits. These are strictly necessary, functional cookies managed through our authentication provider — they don't track your browsing activity for advertising purposes, and ROOK does not currently use third-party advertising or ad-tracking cookies.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Since ROOK's cookies are used to keep you signed in, blocking them will mean you need to log in again each time you visit.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact information listed on our website.
