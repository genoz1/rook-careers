# Search territory coverage v1

Coverage is computed at search time. It does not update jobs or create job coordinates.
Explicit employer coverage clauses override title/default regions; unknown directional qualifiers and exclusions fail closed. Generic Remote grants no coverage. City points retain their own mileage. Search responses bind containment to the actual query coordinates.

`boundaries.json` is generated by `node backend/territory/buildBoundaries.js` from US Atlas 3.0.1 / Census 2017 1:10m geography. These are general-purpose county/state boundaries, not employer-drawn sales boundaries. Four decimal degree precision is retained. No runtime geometry packages or external calls are required. See https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas .

South Texas uses the Texas Governor's 46-county South Texas definition, which includes Bexar/San Antonio: https://gov.texas.gov/uploads/files/business/Regional_Profile_South_Texas.pdf .

Other named regions are explicit ROOK search conventions, not claims of universal employer boundaries. The complete county lists live in the generator and state lists in coverage.js. Central Valley is the 18-county Sacramento/San Joaquin valley county convention (including county portions outside the physical valley). Southern California is the ten southern counties; Northern California is the remainder. Florida uses the listed Central and South county groups, with North the remainder. Southern Nevada uses Clark, Esmeralda, Lincoln and Nye; Northern is the remainder. These defaults can be narrowed or expanded by explicit source coverage. Ambiguous unrecognized subregions do not fall back to their entire state.

The county-based convention necessarily has coarser scope than a precise employer-specific boundary; an employer's explicit territory definition takes precedence. Do not add company-name exceptions or substitute centroids. Regenerate geometry if definitions change.
