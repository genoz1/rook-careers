## What is implemented

Three posting slots/week/channel; seven-day queue; 23 reusable guidance items with 30-day duplicate cooldown. Missing content creates durable backlog, not silent success. Buffer channel discovery is cached for 24 hours; this lane is capped at 50 requests/day and 900/month, in addition to account-wide limits.
- Four deterministic aggregate-only monthly report families, dedicated HTML pages, canonical URLs, sitemap, resource links and GA4 attribution. Freshness, minimum-cell and report-content-hash checks precede promotion. No individual job records are read by the acquisition generator. Existing SEO fixes and ten curated guide improvements remain on this branch.
- Verified-account partner signup and private reporting; signed, expiring first-touch referral cookies; passive authenticated binding; $5 once per new monthly buyer. Historical monthly customer baseline, unique customer/email/card fingerprints, first-invoice verification, 30-day hold, refund/dispute blocks and replay protection. Existing billing routes, Stripe subscription mutations and paywall logic are unchanged.
- Public partner recruitment as the compliant alternative to cold outreach; bounded official-source research, robots checks, domain deduplication and referral-site candidate discovery. Discovery never authorizes email. Verified partners can opt into onboarding; unsubscribe, bounce, complaint and reply suppression are implemented. Follow-ups require a tested inbound reply route. No group spam, personal-profile automation or scraped email campaigns.
- Monthly payout batch preparation, $10 proposed minimum with carryforward, immutable review digest and refund revalidation. **No payment submission adapter is enabled.** Payee onboarding, provider eligibility, tax handling and an approved payout policy are prerequisites to automated disbursement.
- Private revenue dashboard; canonical Stripe observer and daily missed-webhook/refund reconciliation; normalized MRR distinguished from introductory cash receipts and full-price subscriber count; channel-level gross receipts; GA4/GSC read-only adapters; weekly scorecards; SEO issue detection; optional approved owner exception alerts.
